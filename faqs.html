Working together

The questions we get asked most

How will I know if your work is actually making a difference?

Every client receives a visibility dashboard showing rankings, calls, visibility, recommendations, and measurable improvements. You'll never have to guess whether your marketing is working, you'll see exactly what's improving every month.

How is Rank Proxi different from a typical SEO agency?

Most SEO agencies focus on rankings. We focus on recommendations. Our system helps strengthen the trust signals Google and AI use when deciding which businesses to recommend. Every month you'll know exactly what's improving, why it matters, and what we're working on next.

We've hired an SEO agency before. Why would this be different?

Many clients come to us after disappointing SEO experiences. We don't hide behind technical reports or vague updates. We explain what we're doing, why we're doing it, and how it contributes to your visibility and customer acquisition.

How long does it take to see results?

Some improvements can be seen within the first month, but meaningful gains typically take 60–90 days. Local visibility compounds over time as trust signals strengthen.

Do you guarantee #1 rankings?

No. No one can honestly guarantee #1 rankings because Google controls the results. What we do guarantee is a structured process, measurable progress, transparent reporting, and a strategy focused on improving your visibility where customers are searching.

Will I understand what you're doing each month?

Yes. We explain every recommendation in plain English. You'll know what's improving, what's holding you back, and what comes next, without needing to understand SEO terminology.

Do I need to change my website?

Not always. We start by identifying what's limiting your visibility. Sometimes improvements can be made to your existing website, while other situations may require additional optimization. We'll explain exactly what matters before making recommendations.

Is this just for Google, or does it help with AI tools like ChatGPT too?

Our methodology focuses on the trust signals that influence both traditional local search and AI-powered recommendations. As search evolves, we help businesses strengthen the signals that improve their chances of being discovered across both.

What happens after I become a client?

After onboarding, we audit your current visibility, identify the highest-impact opportunities, prioritize improvements, and begin implementing our Recommendation System. You'll receive regular updates and access to your dashboard so you can see what's changing and why.

Is there a contract, and can I cancel?

No lock-in. All plans are month-to-month and you can cancel any time with 30 days' notice. You can also upgrade or downgrade at any point, we'll adjust the strategy and the billing immediately.

Still have a question?

Ask it directly. We'll reply within 2 hours during business hours, with a real answer, not a sales script.
