Methodology

How we measure results

Transparency matters. Here is exactly where every number on this page comes from, and why we deliberately under-report.

Google Business Profile data

Profile views, phone calls, website clicks and direction requests are pulled directly from Google Business Profile performance reports, the same numbers Google shows the business owner in their own dashboard. Not estimated, not projected.

AI search rankings

Keyword positions are tracked and recorded the way they appear in Google and AI-generated search overviews, including ranking position, competitor count and star rating at the time of the search.

Monthly trend data

Month-by-month breakdowns are synced straight from Google Business Profile. Every figure is actual recorded data from the platform, not an average or an extrapolation.

Conservative by design

Google calls and clicks are exact. Website calls are conservatively calculated from verified traffic using an observed 5–7% call rate. We show you the minimum, so the real number is always higher, never lower.

The we'd rather lose a client than lie guarantee

Every number above is either pulled from Google's own data or conservatively calculated from verified traffic. If you ever find a single misleading number in our case studies, tell us, and we'll refund you. No arguments.
